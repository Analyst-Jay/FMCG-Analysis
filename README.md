# FMCG Sales & Distribution Performance Analysis

## 📌 Project Overview

This project presents an interactive Power BI dashboard developed to analyse sales and distribution performance within a Fast-Moving Consumer Goods (FMCG) business. The dashboard provides a consolidated view of sales performance, product contribution, distributor performance, sales-force productivity, outlet coverage, and profitability.

The report is designed to help stakeholders monitor business performance, identify areas that require attention, and make more informed, data-driven decisions.

## 🎯 Business Problem

FMCG businesses operate across multiple products, distributors, sales representatives, and distribution channels. Without a clear and consolidated reporting system, monitoring sales performance, evaluating target achievement, understanding product contribution, and assessing distribution effectiveness can become difficult.

This project addresses that challenge by presenting key sales and distribution metrics through an interactive dashboard that supports business performance monitoring and exploration.

## 🎯 Project Objectives

- Analyse overall sales performance and available sales trends.
- Evaluate product performance and contribution to sales.
- Compare performance across distributors.
- Monitor sales-representative performance.
- Examine outlet coverage and distribution productivity.
- Analyse gross profit and other available profitability indicators.
- Present business performance through an interactive, multi-page Power BI report.

## 🛠️ Tools & Technologies

- **Microsoft Power BI:** Dashboard development, visualisation, and interactive reporting.
- **DAX:** Measures and calculations used to support business performance analysis.
- **Power Query:** Data preparation and transformation.
- **Data Modelling:** Organising relationships between sales data and relevant business dimensions.

## 📊 Dashboard Overview

The report contains multiple analytical pages, each focusing on a different aspect of FMCG sales and distribution performance.

### 1. Sales Performance Analysis

This section provides an overview of sales performance and the available performance indicators. It supports the monitoring of sales results and comparison against targets where applicable.

### 2. Product Performance Analysis

This section examines product-level performance to help identify differences in sales contribution across the available product categories or items.

### 3. Distributor Performance Analysis

This section compares distributor performance to support the evaluation of distribution contributions and highlight areas for further investigation.

### 4. Sales Force Performance Analysis

This section focuses on the performance of sales representatives and supports the assessment of sales-force productivity across the business.

### 5. Coverage & Productivity Analysis

This section examines outlet coverage and distribution productivity, helping stakeholders explore how effectively sales activities reach the available outlets.

### 6. Gross Profit & Profitability Analysis

This section focuses on gross profit and the profitability indicators available in the report, providing an additional perspective beyond sales volume and revenue.

## 🔍 Analytical Approach

The project uses a structured business intelligence approach:

1. **Data preparation:** Preparing the available data for analysis.
2. **Data transformation:** Organising data into a suitable structure for reporting.
3. **Data modelling:** Establishing relationships required for analysis across business dimensions.
4. **Measure development:** Using relevant calculations to support sales, target, and profitability reporting.
5. **Dashboard development:** Presenting the analysis through interactive visuals and KPI summaries.
6. **Performance exploration:** Reviewing results across products, distributors, sales representatives, and outlet coverage.

## 💡 Business Value

The dashboard provides a consolidated reporting experience that can help stakeholders:

- Monitor sales performance from a single reporting interface.
- Compare product and distributor contributions.
- Review sales-force performance and productivity.
- Examine outlet coverage and distribution effectiveness.
- Include profitability in sales performance discussions.
- Identify areas requiring additional investigation and management attention.

## 📁 Project Deliverable

The main deliverable is an interactive, multi-page Power BI report that brings together the project's sales and distribution analysis in one place.

The report is intended to support performance monitoring, comparison across business dimensions, and further investigation of sales and distribution patterns.

## ⚠️ Limitations

The conclusions available from this project depend on the coverage, quality, and definitions of the underlying data. Dashboard results should be interpreted within the reporting period and business context represented by the dataset.

The report supports performance analysis but does not independently establish the causes of observed business patterns.

By bringing together sales performance, product analysis, distributor evaluation, sales-force productivity, outlet coverage, and profitability, the dashboard provides a practical foundation for exploring business performance and supporting data-informed decision-making.
